# Galactic Transport, Politics, Economy and World Engine Optimization

## Description
Comprehensive architectural review, critique, optimization, and deep enhancement of the `.agents` folder for The Stellar Confluence Universe. Engineered a 4-tier galactic transport taxonomy and mobility engine, an interstellar politics, governance, and sociology engine, a 25+ commodity multi-currency trade economy, 25+ multi-stage adventure quest blueprints, and enriched Grade 4–6 child-accessible storytelling with multidisciplinary scientific and philosophical authenticity, validated through 131 automated sanity tests in ~500ms with 100% health.

## Progress
* **4-Tier Galactic Transport & Multi-Scale Mobility Engine (`galactic_transport_engine.py`)**: Built a complete taxonomy covering Tier 1 (Atmospheric Skimmers, Sand-Sails, Benthic Crawlers, Planetary Mag-Levs, Skyhooks), Tier 2 (Solar-Sail Cutters, Ion Freighters, Coronal Plasma Scoopers, Cycler Stations), Tier 3 (Harmonic Wave-Riders, Void Phase Shuttles, Tachyon Slipstream Corvettes, Dyson Laser Accelerators), Tier 4 (Dark-Matter Drift Caravans, Keystone Stargates), and Cultural Faction Craft, calculating propulsion kinetics, load factors, and cockpit sensory experiences.
* **Interstellar Politics, Governance & Sociology Engine (`galactic_sociology_politics_engine.py`)**: Modeled 7 governance charters (Solar Hegemony, Monastic Enclaves, Clockwork Syndicates, Flotilla Parliaments, Deep-Core Collectives, Crystal Choirs, Bio-Communes), social strata, rites of passage, hospitality customs, sacred cosmic taboos, and bilateral treaties.
* **Dynamic Trade Economy & Multi-Currency System (`galactic_trade_economy.py`)**: Upgraded to 25+ commodities across 4 tiers (Raw Ores, Refined Tech, Biotech, Luxury Relics), 4 galactic currencies (Sol-Credits, Guild Scrip, Void-Tokens, Flotilla Vouchers), dynamic price elasticity, convoy scheduling, and planetary stockpiles.
* **Procedural Scale, Quests & Storytelling Upgrades (`galactic_scale_generator.py`, `galactic_adventure_engine.py`, `story_generator.py`)**: Added transport infrastructure to procedural star systems, expanded quest archetypes to 25+ rich scenarios, and upgraded 3-act story generation to seamlessly weave vehicle flight physics, governance accords, and creature handling into sensory, warm, Grade 4–6 accessible prose.
* **Master CLI Hub & Diagnostic Suite Expansion (`agent_hub.py`, `core/agent_core.py`, `agent_self_test.py`)**: Added `hub.py transport`, `hub.py politics`, `hub.py sociology`, and `hub.py economy` subcommands, enhanced global search, and expanded in-process tests from 102 to **131 sanity checks** running in ~500ms with 100% PASS rate.
