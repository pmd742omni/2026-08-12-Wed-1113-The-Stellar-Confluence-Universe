# Comprehensive Universe Engine Optimization & Dual-Layer Authoring Framework

## Description
Deeply optimized, elevated, and rigorously verified the .agents agentic orchestration system for The Stellar Confluence Universe. Added procedural full planetary systems with Goldilocks habitable zones and Keplerian periods, cosmic megastructure anomalies (Dyson swarms, quartz nebulae), 4-tier multiscale mobility journey simulations with everyday physical metaphors, bilateral diplomatic peace summits with gift exchanges, interstellar trade route arbitrage analysis with intuitive economic principles, and dual-layer authoring/evaluations serving both young readers (Grade 4-6 readability) and smart thinkers. Expanded the automated test suite to 151 sanity checks with 100% PASS in ~1.5 seconds.
